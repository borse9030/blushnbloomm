About Bloom&blush

Welcome to Bloom&blush, an intimate boutique gifting and floral studio founded by Siddhi Kokate, based in Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune.

Born out of a deep reverence for the beauty of Indian celebrations and a passion for tactile craftsmanship, Bloom&blush bridges traditional ceremonial gifting with contemporary editorial elegance. We understand that whether it's a groom's grand baraat garland, a bespoke engagement ring tray, or a luxurious festive hamper for family, each creation carries immense emotion.

Unlike commercial factories or mass-market websites, every single Bloom&blush arrangement is personally styled, meticulously crafted, and quality-inspected by Siddhi Kokate. We work closely with our clients over WhatsApp to understand their color palette, preferred flowers, family traditions, and sentimental nuances.

Uncompromising Craftsmanship

We use damage-free note-folding methods for money garlands and the freshest, premium botanical stems.

About Bloom&blush

Welcome to Bloom&blush, an intimate boutique gifting and floral studio founded by Siddhi Kokate, based in Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune.

Born out of a deep reverence for the beauty of Indian celebrations and a passion for tactile craftsmanship, Bloom&blush bridges traditional ceremonial gifting with contemporary editorial elegance.

Every arrangement is personally styled, meticulously crafted, and quality-inspected to ensure it carries immense emotion.

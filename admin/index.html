Recommended Free Cloud Storage for Videos & Photos

Hosting high-definition videos and large photos directly on small servers can cause slow load times or expensive bandwidth bills. Below are the top 100% free options to host your boutique media smoothly:

YouTube (Unlisted) 100% Free • Unlimited

Best for: Achievement videos, reels, and craft masterclasses of any length or file size.

  1. Upload your video on YouTube Studio.
  2. Set visibility to "Unlisted" (only visible when embedded on your website).
  3. Copy the link (e.g. https://youtu.be/...) and paste it into the achievement video field.
  4. Bonus: Cover photo is automatically generated!
Open YouTube Studio →
Cloudinary 25 GB Free / Month

Best for: High-res photography, short video clips, and direct MP4 / WebP streaming with auto-compression.

  1. Sign up for a free account at Cloudinary.com.
  2. Upload your photos or video clips in the Media Library.
  3. Click "Copy URL" and paste directly into Bloom&blush!
Visit Cloudinary →
Firebase Storage 5 GB Free Included

Best for: Built-in storage directly inside your existing Google Firebase project (blushnbloomm-4c7b9).

  1. Open your Firebase Console Storage.
  2. Upload files into a media/ folder.
  3. Get the public download URL and paste into your creation or achievement.
Open Firebase Console →
ImgBB (Instant Photos) 100% Free • No Card

Best for: Instant photo uploads up to 32 MB with direct image links.

  1. Go to ImgBB.com.
  2. Drop your image and select "Don't autodelete".
  3. Under Embed codes, select "Direct links" and paste here.
Visit ImgBB →

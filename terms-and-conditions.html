2. Money Garlands & Currency Integrity Policy

Bloom&blush is celebrated across Pune and Maharashtra for crafting luxury groom currency garlands (Note Haar), currency bouquets, and shagun origami bouquets using authentic Indian currency notes. Our currency crafting strictly adheres to the highest legal and ethical standards:

The Bloom&blush Zero-Damage Currency Pledge

In strict compliance with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Clean Note Policy, our studio guarantees that NO currency note is ever stapled, pierced with pins, cut, punctured, glued, taped, or chemically defaced. Every single banknote is secured using non-invasive acid-free sleeves, archival tension bands, or precision origami folding. After the wedding ceremony, all banknotes can be detached smoothly without damage and spent or deposited as 100% valid legal tender.

  • Currency Provision Options:
    • Client-Supplied Notes: Clients may provide crisp, uncirculated bank notes directly at our Pune studio. Notes are counted, verified, and logged in the client's presence or via photo/video confirmation.
    • Studio Procurement Assistance: Upon client request and subject to bank availability, Bloom&blush can facilitate fresh bank bundles on your behalf. Currency face value must be transferred 100% upfront via bank transfer or UPI.
  • Pricing Transparency Breakdown: Every money garland quotation is divided into two distinct components: (1) Currency Face Value (pass-through exact sum of notes used, e.g. ₹5,000, ₹10,000, ₹25,000, or ₹50,000), and (2) Studio Artistry & Crafting Fee (silk ribbons, metallic zari, brocade neckbands, velvet accent florals, and protective storage box).
  • Counting & Handover Protocol: Before sealing any currency creation, the studio conducts a dual-count video log showing the exact note quantity. Upon local delivery or studio pickup, the client or authorized representative must sign or WhatsApp-acknowledge receipt of the verified count.

3. Artisanal Fresh Floral Bouquets & Perishable Care

Fresh flowers are living, perishable botanicals sourced directly from select flower farms in Maharashtra, Bangalore, and international Dutch flower auctions.

Seasonal Substitution Rule

Specific blooms (e.g. peonies, ranunculus, imported hydrangeas) are subject to weather and market availability. If a bloom is unavailable, we substitute with flowers of equal or higher value adhering strictly to your color palette.

24–48 Hour Booking Notice

Bridal bouquets and celebration florals require at least 24 to 48 hours advance booking so our florists can condition blooms to reach peak aesthetic opening at your event.

Post-Delivery Hydration Care

Fresh bouquets travel with a temporary water reservoir or hydration wrap. Once received, trim stems at a 45° angle, place in cool fresh water, and keep away from direct sunlight or AC airflow.

Because cut flowers naturally age over 3 to 7 days depending on room temperature and hydration care, Bloom&blush cannot be held liable for premature wilting resulting from flowers being kept in unconditioned cars, under intense stage spotlights, or without fresh water after delivery.

5. Botanical Resin Flower & Varmala Preservation

Preserving wedding varmalas, bridal garlands, and sentimental anniversary florals in crystal-clear epoxy resin is a delicate, multi-step scientific and artistic process:

Essential Resin Preservation Understanding

Natural Color Evolution: Flowers undergo specialized chemical-free dehydration before resin casting. Natural pigments evolve during dehydration: deep red roses naturally darken to vintage wine/burgundy tones; bright white flowers transition to soft ivory or antique cream; orchids may shift slightly in translucency. This organic chromatic transition is a natural hallmark of botanical science, not a flaw.

  • Handover Speed: Wedding varmalas must be handed over to our Pune studio within 24 to 48 hours following the wedding ceremony. Bouquets must be kept in cool, refrigerated conditions (never frozen) prior to handover. Wilted, brown, or decaying blooms cannot be restored.
  • 3 to 4 Week Curing Timeline: Premium resin preservation requires 21 to 30 days for complete moisture extraction, chemical degassing, multi-layer pouring, thermal cure cycling, and optical bevel polishing. Rush orders cannot override resin chemical cure times, as rushing risks cloudiness or bubbles.
  • Micro-Bubble Authenticity: Our studio uses commercial vacuum degassing to remove trapped air. However, because real organic flower petals release microscopic air molecules as resin cures around them, minute micro-bubbles may naturally remain near petal textures. These celebrate authentic handmade casting.
  • Resin Care: Keep your finished keepsake away from direct outdoor UV sunlight and heat sources to maintain optical clarity. Dust gently using the microfiber cloth provided.

In accordance with modern consumer protection frameworks and Indian commercial law:

  • Exclusion of Bespoke & Perishable Returns: Under Section 2(47) of the Consumer Protection Act, goods made to the consumer's personalized specifications (customized names, monogrammed tags, date-specific florals, folded currency garlands, dried memory resin) or goods which are liable to deteriorate rapidly (fresh flowers) are strictly exempt from standard return and refund policies.
  • Order Cancellation by Client:
    • Before Production: If cancelled more than 7 days prior to the event date and before materials/flowers have been procured, the advance deposit may be converted into a studio credit voucher valid for 6 months.
    • After Production Has Begun: Once flowers are conditioned, currency is folded, acrylic is cut, or resin is poured, orders cannot be cancelled and deposits are non-refundable.
  • Wedding Date Rescheduling: In the event of unforeseen wedding date changes, Bloom&blush will accommodate the new date free of charge provided written notice is given at least 5 business days in advance, subject to studio calendar availability. For fresh flowers, additional charges may apply if replacement flowers must be repurchased for the revised date.
